# SIRYUS Ecosystem Design System

## Shared principle

The ecosystem is connected by care, clear navigation and subtle SIRYUS attribution. It is not connected by one theme. Each brand owns its typography, palette, image language, page rhythm and motion.

## SIRYUS

- Role: the doorway into four different worlds.
- Type: Neue Machina only.
- Palette: white and black as the frame. Brand imagery supplies color.
- Signature: four full-height mirror shards. The shards are the links, not decoration behind another call to action.
- Motion: one staggered shard arrival and a direct expansion on hover or focus.

## SIRYUS A.M

- Role: artist management, marketing, development and music projects.
- Type: Cenzo for expressive display text, Arial for direct reading text.
- Palette: warm paper, black, electric blue, coral and yellow.
- Image language: large artist portraits, hard crops and music-editorial scale.
- Motion: a single hero rise and image reveal. Content is visible without motion.

## SIGNAL. by SIRYUS

- Role: story-led marketing and growth agency.
- Type: DM Serif Display for story and Manrope for systems and navigation.
- Palette: warm stone, cream, black and SIGNAL. red. No grey text.
- Image language: people making, arranging, presenting and testing ideas.
- Motion: one controlled image reveal and a reduced-motion-safe signal orbit.

## ARC. Creatives

ARC retains its existing approved design. Extraction into `apps/arc` must be mechanical. Do not restyle its typography, colors, imagery, layout or motion.

## Interface rules

- No eyebrow headings, pills, decorative horizontal rules, em dashes or grey body text.
- Copy is simple, direct and human.
- Focus is visible, touch targets are at least 44 pixels, and key content is present before JavaScript.
- Motion stops or simplifies when reduced motion is requested.
