## Parent concept
- Navigation label: `SIRYUS` (or approved parent logo).
- Hero H1: `Different Worlds. One Creative Universe.`
- Subhead: `Independent ideas. Distinct identities. Connected by a belief in what creativity can become.` This is **proposed editorial copy**; revise for natural tone and truthfulness.
- CTA: `Explore the universe`, anchor to venture selection.
- SIRYUS A.M card: `For independent artists building lasting music careers.`
- ARC. Creatives card: `A place for faith-rooted creatives to grow, collaborate and create.`
- SIGNAL. card: `Marketing shaped by stories that people remember.`
- PAVO Create card: `Independent software products and digital experiences.` External link, show off-site affordance.
- About focus: parent purpose, legal context, independent venture relationships; no spurious agency services list.
- Footer: `© 2026 Siryus Creative Media Ltd. All rights reserved.` Add legal links, privacy/contact, no fictional office locations.

## SIRYUS A.M copy cues
- H1 direction: `Make independent music move further.` (proposal, not approved slogan).
- Present A.M as a creative collective and a serious artist-focused business. Distinguish direct management from open services. Services are not an offer of guaranteed streams or placements.
- Roster: Max Prodigy, Jlinez only. Former managed artists and future signees must never appear as currently signed.
- Projects: Indiba Project Series; other entries only if verified. Acoustic Rwanda absent.
- Include DSP release index with verified real links and metadata.

## ARC. Creatives copy cues
- H1 direction: `Room to grow. People to grow with.` (proposal).
- Source language: `Ascent. Refuge. Collective.` Christian creative community; excellence without performance pressure; formation, collaboration and faith-rooted artistry.
- Do not portray ARC as a generic agency. Include music releases and events even if audience is broader than music.

## SIGNAL. copy cues
- Identity line: `SIGNAL. by SIRYUS`.
- Candidate positioning: `A marketing agency built around the stories worth telling.`
- Primary H1: `Make your message matter.`
- Intro: `We find the story at the heart of your brand, build a strategy around it, and turn it into marketing people can feel and act on.`
- Three-step approach: `Find the story. Shape the strategy. Move the message.`
- Services: story and brand strategy; messaging and copy; marketing campaigns; email/CRM; content and creative; growth/measurement. Validate every exact service with the user before publishing as an active offer.
- Work section must show verified client work only; if none is publicly approved, focus on methods and illustrative, clearly labeled examples, not fabricated case studies.
- CTA: `Tell us what you're building.` / `Let's Talk`.

## Writing standards
Simple, conversational, clear, human. Avoid em dashes, padded agency clichés and fake numbers. Write accessible headings and concise meaningful sections. No generic filler paragraphs. The user prefers grade 6–7 readability for nontechnical writing.
