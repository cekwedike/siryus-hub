# Brand distinctions

## Siryus Creative Media Ltd

This is the parent company and ecosystem owner. Never describe it as a holding company. On the parent site, its job is to explain the family of brands and help people choose where to go.

## SIRYUS A.M

SIRYUS A.M is the artist management brand. Its current managed roster is exactly:

- Max Prodigy
- Jlinez

Hidaya is legacy. Content may be archived for history, but it must not imply a current management relationship. Winny is future. Do not publish a current-roster claim until the user confirms it. Acoustic Rwanda must be removed from the new public experience.

## ARC. Creatives

Use the full name `ARC. Creatives` in headings, descriptions, metadata, and general prose. Existing `/arc` content can remain during the first phase, but its voice, content types, and visual system should stay distinct from the parent doorway.

## PAVO

PAVO is the product name. Do not label the product `PAVO Create`. `PAVO Create Ltd` is only for legal contexts. The parent-site card links to `https://pavocreate.com/`; no PAVO page, subdirectory, or duplicate product copy is built in this repository.

## SIGNAL.

The wordmark includes the full stop. Its identity is pending. Until approval:

- Use a quiet typographic treatment.
- Do not invent a logo, colors, imagery, slogan, audience, or product promise.
- Mark it as taking shape in simple language.
- Do not add a dead link.

## Shared visual rules

- No eyebrow headers.
- No pill navigation, pill badges, or rounded tag chips.
- No horizontal line dividers unless requested.
- No em dashes in visible copy.
- Use real brand assets where approved. Do not force all brands into one visual skin.
- Keep the parent page calm enough that each brand can remain distinct.
- Use useful motion only, and respect reduced motion.

## Copy test

Every sentence should answer at least one of these questions: What is this brand? Who is it for? What can I do next? If it does none of these, remove or rewrite it.
